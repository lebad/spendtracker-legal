1. Who is responsible

SpendTracker is an independent application for organizing information from bank notification emails. Publisher: [PUBLISHER NAME TO BE CONFIRMED]. Country and contact address: [TO BE CONFIRMED]. Privacy contact: [PUBLIC CONTACT EMAIL TO BE CONFIRMED].

2. Information the app uses

When you connect Gmail, the app uses your email address and connection credentials to find messages matching the configured bank-email filter. It imports message identifiers, dates, subjects, and message content, including financial information contained in those emails. This can include amounts, currencies, merchants, transaction dates, card identifiers, account numbers, and reported balances. Matching messages may be retained even if they cannot be parsed into transactions.

The app also stores information you enter, such as card owner names, category rules, transaction edits and splits, budgets, recurring bills, savings entries, and preferences. Local diagnostic logs may contain sync status, errors, message identifiers, and search details.

3. Why information is processed

The app uses this information to import and categorize transactions, calculate spending summaries, display your budgets and savings, synchronize your data across your devices, and troubleshoot import problems. Automatic categorization and calculations are informational; they do not make lending, credit, or other decisions about your eligibility for services.

If you contact the publisher, the publisher receives the contact details and information you choose to send and uses them to answer your request. Do not send passwords, full bank statements, or unnecessary financial information.

4. Gmail access and credentials

The standard connection uses a Google app password stored in the device Keychain and connects to Google over an encrypted connection. The app is designed to read matching mail; it does not send, move, or delete messages. An app password is not technically restricted to just bank emails, so the app's filtering should not be confused with a mailbox permission boundary.

If a version offers Google sign-in, it uses Google authorization and read-only Gmail access instead. Authorization tokens are stored using the app's credential storage. Google processes your account information under its own policies.

5. Local storage and iCloud

Imported emails, transactions, financial entries, and preferences are stored on your device. When iCloud is available and configured, this data can synchronize through your private iCloud database to devices using your Apple Account. Dashboard layout remains local. Mail credentials are kept in the device Keychain and are not included in the app's financial-data sync.

The inspected app does not send your mailbox or financial records to a publisher-operated server and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. This does not prevent Google and Apple from processing information needed to provide Gmail and iCloud. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy. Their services may process information in multiple countries.

6. Retention, disconnecting, and deletion

Saved data remains available between sessions. Disconnecting email removes the app's saved mail credentials and stops new imports; it does not delete previously imported emails, transactions, or edits. You can also revoke the app password or Google authorization in your Google Account.

The current app does not provide a single control that permanently erases every local and iCloud copy. Removing the app should not be assumed to remove iCloud data, backups, preserved legacy files, or all local records. These copies must be managed separately through the relevant device and iCloud storage controls. Do not describe re-sync as deletion: it can import the messages again. Contact the publisher for assistance with storage locations and deletion. The publisher cannot directly erase records held only on your devices or in your private iCloud account.

Before release: specify a verified complete deletion procedure and retention criteria for support correspondence. No automatic deletion period for imported records is promised by this draft.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, or obtain a portable copy of personal information, and to complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact the publisher using the contact details above. Acknowledging this policy does not waive your rights or authorize unrelated uses of your data.

Before release: identify applicable laws and the legal basis for each processing purpose. Acceptance of Terms or acknowledgement of this policy is not a substitute for that assessment.

8. Security and website hosting

The app uses platform credential storage and encrypted service connections, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your devices, Google Account, and Apple Account. Notification previews may reveal transaction details depending on your settings.
